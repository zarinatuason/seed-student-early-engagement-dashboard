PURPOSE

SEED helps a Student Advisor:
- Identify learner–paper records requiring attention.
- Understand the reasons and supporting academic evidence.
- Examine the learner’s situation across enrolled papers.
- Contact the learner and read their response.
- Decide and record appropriate support.
- Track outstanding actions and follow-ups.

The Student Advisor makes support decisions. Everyone testing SEED acts only as a Student Advisor.
