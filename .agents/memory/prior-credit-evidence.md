---
name: Prior-credit evidence semantics
description: How to explain the supplied snapshot without silently changing its aggregate definitions.
---

Preserve supplied academic aggregates and classifications. Identify prior-credit detail rows as historical credit, not current-offering submissions, but do not remove them from the source-provided submission counts or score averages.

**Why:** Cross-source verification showed the supplied aggregates include prior-credit submission records and released results. Removing them from display calculations silently redefines the snapshot. Nominal due counts also include assessments whose availability ended before late registration.

**How to apply:** Show source totals unchanged, disclose their inclusion rules, and show learner registration opportunity and prior-credit labels alongside individual evidence. Obtain source-owner confirmation of underlying thresholds or intended participation interpretation instead of inventing a revised risk model.
